One dad joke, at most 200 characters, for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s "Dad Joke Showdown" bounty, $5 in USDC. A judge model reads the jokes two at a time and picks the one that lands the bigger groan.

It writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — a father, his houseplants, and the modern curse of being left on read:

> I asked Dad why he talks to his plants every morning. 'They're the only ones in this house who never leaf me on read.'

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
